Events, news and analysts

Every point on the map keeps the sources that were read (fonti[]): news agencies, newspapers, official statements, analysis centres and OSINT accounts. Only facts, date and link are reported, with at most a short excerpt. The daily news comes from RSS feeds (Google News, UKMTO, JMIC, MARAD and others): headline, date, publisher and link only. Analysts’ YouTube videos are their authors’ opinions: only the title, a short description and the link are stored.
