Geo-economic Risk Monitor

An open-source observatory of how a geopolitical event turns into a cost: event → chokepoint or infrastructure → flows of ships and energy → freight rates → prices → who is exposed.

  1. Read the map: events, chokepoints (the magenta diamonds), energy, cables, ports. Switch layers on and off in the panel on the left.
  2. Tap a chokepoint: measured ship transits per day (IMF PortWatch), what passes through, who is exposed and the linked prices.
  3. Open the Terminal for prices, the calendar, freight, stocks, countries and news, and the Reports for the analysis.

Every data point carries its source, date and reliability. Updated automatically every day. Some source descriptions are in Italian. A personal project by Gabriele Giuliani, built with the help of an AI assistant.
