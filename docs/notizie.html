    How it works. Every day the script raccolta/notizie/testate.py reads the public feeds of about 60 outlets (general news from the US, UK, EU, Northern Europe and Italy; maritime and energy trade press; think tanks). It keeps only the headline, a short feed summary and the link: the text belongs to the publisher. Topics are assigned with keywords in English, Italian, Danish/Norwegian and Dutch, so they can be wrong. Stories group similar headlines published by different outlets in the last 48 hours. Some outlets are reached through Google News because they block their feeds. “Paywall” means the article may require a subscription.

    Items in the Specialist OSINT sources box come from raccolta/notizie/raccogli.py (maritime advisories, analysis centres, mapping channels) and are raw material still to be verified.
