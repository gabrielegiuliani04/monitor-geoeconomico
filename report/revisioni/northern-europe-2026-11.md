## 3. Corrections in detail

1. **Ports led by weekly noise.** The NORD agent report and the first draft used the 7-day change (Rotterdam −10.8%, «rallenta»). Over 30 days Rotterdam is +2.3% and over the year to date −0.5%. The report now leads with the two longer averages and says why.
2. **GNSS west of 17°E.** The agent report states that all high-level cells were east of 17°E. In the week 2–8 November one cell at 55.8°N 16.96°E (east of Öland) was high (10.7% of 681 aircraft, 4 high days). Text corrected; the watch threshold, which this borderline cell would already trigger, was redefined (3 or more cells west of 17°E, or any west of 15°E) and the change is stated.
3. **Rotterdam bunker sales.** `bunker-emissioni.json` (`calcolato.nota`) says the −23.9% includes LNG at 0.45 t/m³; the totals 5,629,788 and 7,396,485 t are oil plus bio-blends only. With LNG the change is −23.2%; the Ship & Bunker headline in the file's link says −24.9%. The report states the first two; the mislabel is reported here for the file's curator.
4. **Storage slope** computed over 13 days in the draft; now 14 days, as the text says.
5. **Cable count and last incident** now include the Šventoji–Liepāja cable (2 January 2026), recorded only in `baltico-sicurezza.json`; the watch list shows 2 January 2026 as the last incident.
6. **Copenhagen police statement** re-attributed to its source in the data (Wikipedia and sources, reliability medium).
7. **Detentions**: «coastal states have become more active» was illustrated with six events, one of which is a Russian detention; wording fixed.
8. **Norway** is the EU's largest gas supplier in the source, not specifically the largest pipeline supplier.
9. **Key point** on 2025 offshore additions aligned with the body (1.96 GW).
10. **Language**: Italian labels from curated files (auction outcomes, bottleneck indicators, cable incidents, hydrogen projects, risk levels «medio/alto/critico») translated; numbers untouched.
11. **Charts**: the ports chart's axis labels were corrupted by a minus-sign substitution applied to the whole SVG element; wind-chart value labels overlapped the target ticks; the Dutch storage label sat on the line. Fixed.
12. **Transit times**: UNCTAD's 14–24 extra days and the model's 10.5 days are both shown, with the reason (sea days at one speed from one origin vs a range of origins and speeds).
